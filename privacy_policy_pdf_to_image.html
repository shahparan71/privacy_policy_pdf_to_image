Privacy Policy

PDF to Image - Image to PDF
Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Shah Paran ("we", "us", or "our") developed PDF to Image - Image to PDF as a utility application for converting PDF documents to images and images to PDF documents.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how files are processed, and how your information is handled.

Privacy at a glance: PDF to Image - Image to PDF does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not upload your selected PDF or image files to our servers.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell personal information through the app.

The app does not require you to provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Account credentials
  • Contacts
  • Location information
  • Financial or payment information

2. Files and Documents

The main purpose of the app is to convert PDF and image files. When you select a PDF or image from your device, the selected file is processed locally on your device to perform the requested conversion.

Your selected files are not uploaded to our servers and are not transmitted to us for processing.

The app does not access files on your device unless you explicitly select them for a conversion operation.

3. PDF to Image Conversion

When you use the PDF to Image feature, the selected PDF is processed on your device and converted into image files.

Depending on the functionality available in the app, you may select individual pages or convert multiple pages from a PDF. The resulting images are saved according to your actions and Android's file access mechanisms.

4. Image to PDF Conversion

When you use the Image to PDF feature, the images you select are processed locally on your device and combined into a PDF document.

The images selected for conversion are not uploaded to our servers.

5. File Storage

Converted files may be saved on your device when you choose to save them. These files remain under your control and can be managed or deleted using your device's file management functionality.

We do not maintain a remote copy of your converted files.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Because the app does not upload your selected documents to our servers, we do not have access to the content of the PDF or image files that you process using the app.

7. Third-Party Services

The current version of the app does not intentionally transmit your documents or personal information to third-party services for processing.

If third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data collection, processing, and sharing practices before or when those practices become applicable.

8. Permissions

The app may request access to files, photos, or other storage mechanisms when necessary for selecting input files or saving converted files.

These permissions are used only to provide the file conversion functionality of the app.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, microphone, phone calls, or SMS messages for its core functionality.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the application. Since document conversion is performed locally on your device and your files are not uploaded to our servers, we do not maintain a server-side database containing your documents or personal information.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not retain personal information or copies of your documents on our servers.

Files created by the app are stored on your device only when you choose to save them. You can delete those files at any time using your device's file management tools.

11. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Since the app does not require an account or collect personal information, there is no personal profile created for users.
