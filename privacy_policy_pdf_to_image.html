Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF to Image - PDF to JPG ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

Privacy at a glance: PDF to Image - PDF to JPG is designed to process your selected PDF files on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information through the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store personal information through PDF to Image - PDF to JPG.

The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, address, or email address to use its core functionality.

2. PDF Files and Images

The App allows you to select PDF files from your device and convert their pages into image files such as JPG images.

The PDF files you select and the images generated from them are processed on your device. Your documents are not uploaded to our servers by the App.

We do not access PDF files or generated images unless you explicitly select or use them through the App's functionality.

3. File Access

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

The App does not upload your PDF documents or converted images to an external server for processing.

7. Data Security

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any files stored on it.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a server-side copy of your PDF documents or converted images.

Any files created by the App and saved to your device are managed by you. You can delete those files using your device's file manager or other applicable system tools.

9. Children's Privacy
